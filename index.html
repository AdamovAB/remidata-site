Process

How It Works

From brief to delivery.

01

Brief

You tell us what data you need — data type, specifications, volume, demographics, and intended use. We scope the project.

02

Source & Collect

For clinical data, we evaluate hospital and laboratory sources. For human data, our contributor networks record, photograph, or annotate to your specifications.

03

Validate

Quality control, format compliance, metadata validation. For clinical data: de-identification, structuring, and expert review where required.

04

Deliver

Clean, labeled datasets delivered via secure transfer with full documentation, quality reports, and applicable rights documentation.

About

About REMI Data

Custom clinical and human data sourcing for AI development.

REMI Data is a US-based company organizing custom data collection, preparation, and project-specific commercial licensing for AI developers. Our activities encompass clinical datasets sourced through healthcare institutions in Ukraine, as well as human training data — speech, face, motion, and other modalities — collected through contributor networks across multiple countries and continents.

Founded in 2024 by Alexander Adamov, an entrepreneur with 25+ years of business experience in Ukraine and the United States. REMI Data is a member of the NVIDIA Inception program and is based in Miami, Florida.
