Voice Recording Task

⏱ Tinatayang oras / Estimated time: ~30 min

📋 Bago magsimula / Before you start

  • Humanap ng tahimik na lugar — walang musika, TV, o ingay mula sa kalye
    Find a quiet room — no music, TV, street noise
  • Gamitin ang voice recorder ng iyong telepono o computer
    Use your phone or computer voice recorder
  • Hawakan ang telepono 15-20 cm mula sa iyong bibig
    Hold the phone 15-20 cm from your mouth
  • Format: WAV o M4A (kahit ano ang ma-produce ng recorder mo)
    Format: WAV or M4A (whatever your recorder produces)
  • Magsalita ng natural, parang nasa phone call ka
    Speak naturally, as if on a phone call
💡 Tip: Pwede mong i-record ang bawat section bilang hiwalay na file, o lahat sa isa.
You can record each section as a separate file, or all in one — your choice.

1 Alphanumeric — Tagalog (~3 min)

Basahin ang bawat linya na parang nagdi-dictate ka sa telepono. Mag-pause ng 2-3 segundo sa pagitan.
Read each line as if dictating over the phone. Pause 2-3 seconds between lines.

1. R X Y walong daan anim tatlo D
2. Z Z pitumpu't dalawa lima anim U tatlo anim O P siyam
3. dalawampu't walo siyamnapu F H siyam W V pito pitumpu
4. P Z M apat K Q limampu pito dalawampu't pito sero
5. limampu't walo anim triple isa limampu O pito isa
6. lima Y doble dos T isa Z S doble apat
7. lima A K dalawampu't pito Q H N M F C C
8. E U apatnapu't tatlo doble anim dalawa U U
9. walo walo siyamnapu lima anim walumpu labindalawa

2 Customer Service — Tagalog (~8 min)

Isipin mo na ikaw ay isang call center agent. Magsalita ng malaya, mag-improvise. Gumamit ng natural na mga pause.
Imagine you are a call center agent. Speak freely, improvise. Use natural pauses.

a) Pagbati / Greeting:

"Magandang hapon po, salamat sa pagtawag, paano ko po kayo matutulungan?"

b) Verification / Pag-verify:

"Para ma-access po ang iyong account, kailangan ko pong i-verify ang ilang impormasyon..."

c) Reklamo / Complaint:

"Naiintindihan ko po ang inyong frustration, hayaan po ninyong i-review ko ang mga charges..."

d) Paglipat / Transfer:

"Ililipat ko po kayo sa technical department, huwag po kayong mag-hang up..."

e) Naayos na / Problem solved:

"Nakita ko na po ang problema, aayusin ko na po ito ngayon..."

f) Pagsara / Closing:

"May iba pa po ba akong maitutulong sa inyo? Magandang araw po sa inyo."
💡 Huwag basahin nang literal — gamitin ang sarili mong mga salita, mag-improvise ng buong conversation para sa bawat scenario.
Don't read these word for word — use your own words, improvise a full conversation for each scenario.

3 Customer Service — English / Ingles (~8 min)

Pareho lang na mga scenario, pero sa Ingles. Magsalita ng natural — HUWAG itago ang iyong accent.
Same scenarios, but in English. Speak naturally — do NOT try to hide your accent.

a) Greeting:

"Thank you for calling, how may I help you today?"

b) Verification:

"I need to verify your identity. Can you please provide your date of birth and the last four digits of your account number?"

c) Complaint:

"I understand your concern about the charges on your bill. Let me pull up your account and take a look."

d) Transfer:

"I'm going to transfer you to our technical support team. Please stay on the line."

e) Resolution:

"I found the issue. It looks like there was a duplicate charge. I'm processing the refund now."

f) Closing:

"Is there anything else I can help you with? Thank you for calling, have a great day."
💡 Magsalita gamit ang sarili mong mga salita. Ang natural mong accent ang kailangan namin.
Speak in your own words. Your natural accent is exactly what we need.

4 Mga Numero at Address — Tagalog (~5 min)

Sabihin ang bawat item nang malakas, parang nagdi-dictate ka sa operator.
Say each item out loud, as if dictating to an operator.

📞 Mga Telepono / Phone numbers (5 numbers):

"Ang number ko po ay zero nine one seven, lima tatlo dalawa, siyam walo isa apat"

+ Mag-imbento at mag-dictate ng 4 pang numero / Invent and dictate 4 more

🏠 Mga Address / Addresses (4 addresses):

"Number one twenty-three, Rizal Avenue, unit four B, Makati City"

+ Mag-imbento ng 3 pang address / Invent 3 more

📅 Mga Petsa / Dates (4 dates):

"Ika-dalawampu't lima ng Agosto, dalawang libo at dalawampu't anim"

+ Mag-imbento ng 3 pang petsa / Invent 3 more

📧 Email (i-spell ang 3 email / spell 3 emails):

Deletree 3 direcciones de email letra por letra / Spell 3 email addresses letter by letter

5 Mga Numero at Address — English / Ingles (~5 min)

Pareho lang, pero sa Ingles.
Same as above, but in English.

📞 Phone numbers (4):

"My phone number is three zero five, four five two, seven eight nine one"

+ Invent 3 more

🏠 Direcciones / Addresses (4):

"One twenty-three Main Street, apartment four B, Miami, Florida, three three one two seven"

+ Invent 3 more

📅 Dates (4):

"August twenty-fifth, twenty twenty-six"

+ Invent 3 more

📧 Email (spell 3):

Spell 3 email addresses letter by letter

6 Malayang Pagsasalita / Free Speech (~5 min)

Magsalita ng malaya tungkol sa kahit ano: ang araw mo, trabaho, hobbies, pagkain, weekend plans.
Talk freely about anything: your day, work, hobbies, food, weekend plans.

  • ~3 minuto sa Tagalog / ~3 minutes in Tagalog
  • ~2 minuto sa Ingles / ~2 minutes in English
💡 Magsalita lang ng natural. Mga pause, pag-aalinlangan, pag-correct sa sarili — lahat okay lang.
Just talk naturally. Pauses, hesitations, self-corrections — all perfectly fine.
