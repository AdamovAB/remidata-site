Retrospective + Prospective

Historical Archives or
Prospective Collection

Two approaches to sourcing the clinical data you need.

Retrospective

Existing clinical archives can be inventoried, digitized, structured and matched against buyer requirements. REMI works with healthcare institutions to identify cases that meet the buyer's cohort definition, extract and structure the relevant data, de-identify records and prepare them for delivery.

Prospective

If the required dataset does not already exist, REMI can organize new collection through participating healthcare providers using a buyer-defined protocol, devices, software and metadata requirements. Cases are collected, tracked and delivered as they meet the agreed specification.

Example: Clinical image → Biopsy / Excision → Pathology → Linked case metadata
