Collection Models

Retrospective & Prospective
Data Collection

Two sourcing models for custom oncology datasets.

Retrospective Collection

Potential assessment of existing hospital archives and records:

  • Archived pathology material
  • Existing hospital records
  • Historical diagnostic imaging
  • Treatment histories
  • Recorded follow-up
  • Available outcome data
  • Digitization and abstraction requirements

Prospective Collection

Potential organization of buyer-defined protocols:

  • Inclusion/exclusion criteria
  • Clinical data collection
  • Pathology and image acquisition
  • Structured metadata
  • Medical specialist involvement
  • Quality assurance
  • Follow-up procedures

Long-term survival data generally require existing longitudinal records or future follow-up periods. Immediate delivery of outcomes requiring years of observation should not be assumed.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can REMI Data source oncology datasets across different cancer indications?
REMI Data evaluates buyer-defined sourcing requirements across solid tumors, hematologic malignancies, and skin cancers. Potential sourcing configurations vary by diagnosis, required data modalities, available medical records, and clinical endpoints. Each project begins with a feasibility assessment through our Ukrainian institutional network.
Can pathology images be linked to clinical records and treatment outcomes?
Patient-level linkage of pathology, clinical records, treatment and outcomes is a core capability we evaluate for each project. Linkage feasibility, completeness and data availability depend on the specific institutions, cancer type and requested clinical endpoints.
Can datasets include molecular, imaging, and clinical modalities?
Multimodal datasets combining pathology, medical imaging, clinical records, molecular data, treatment histories and outcomes can be explored. Actual availability of each modality depends on the specific institutions, patient populations and data systems involved.
Does REMI support retrospective and prospective collection?
Both models are supported. Retrospective collection draws on archived hospital records, pathology materials and imaging. Prospective collection organizes new data capture under buyer-defined protocols. Long-term survival data generally require existing longitudinal records or future follow-up periods.
How is patient-level data linkage evaluated?
Linkage feasibility is assessed for each requested cohort based on the institution's record systems, patient identifiers, data completeness and available modalities. Complete multimodal linkage is not guaranteed for every case — missingness and completeness are reported as part of the feasibility assessment.
How is eligible cohort availability established?
After receiving buyer specifications, REMI evaluates potential Ukrainian hospital and laboratory sources against the requested cancer indication, cohort size, data modalities, clinical endpoints and inclusion criteria. Feasibility findings are reported before any collection commitment.
Can the data be used for commercial AI training?
Commercial AI-training rights are assessed and documented for each specific project. REMI does not assume that access to clinical data or de-identification alone establishes commercial licensing rights. Rights, consent and legal basis are verified with each data source.
How does a custom oncology sourcing project begin?
Contact REMI Data with your cancer indication, required data modalities, target cohort size, clinical endpoints and intended AI application. We evaluate source feasibility, provide a structured assessment, and proceed to collection only after agreement on scope, governance and commercial terms.
